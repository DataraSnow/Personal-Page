01 // The Premise

Star Wars usually shows you the famous Jedi — the duels, the speeches, the dramatic falls. This is the other shelf.

Saelith Veyra is a Jedi the Council never quite knows what to do with: Echani, intelligence-trained, running cover identities since she was fourteen. She doesn't fight battles. She works rooms. She turns assets.

"She didn't romanticize the Jedi tradition. She used what worked, and she paid for everything."

The novel begins on Ilum at thirteen and ends on the night Anakin marches on the Temple.

03 // Archive Maker

I've been a Star Wars fan since I was small enough that my world was mostly carpet. Saelith started as a single sentence I couldn't get out of my head: what if the most interesting Jedi in the room was the one nobody noticed?

This is my long-term portfolio project. I'm using Saelith to learn how to publish across mediums — prose, illustration, comics — and to build something I'm proud of.

— Datara
